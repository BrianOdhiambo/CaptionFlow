# Co-designer test script — CaptionFlow

Run this with a Deaf KSL co-designer against the live demo (http://127.0.0.1:8787).
Goal is not to "pass" — it is to find what makes captions hard to follow.
Record what breaks in their own words, not yours.

Before you start, note:
- Who (first name or initials)
- Date, time
- The video you used
- KSL as first language? (yes/no)

---

## Warm-up

1. Pick a video, let captions generate, watch it once through. Do not change anything.
   - Ask: could you follow the story? What did you miss?

## Text size

2. Find a caption you had to strain to read. Make it bigger until it is comfortable.
   - Ask: is this comfortable now? Too big? Too small?

## Timing / pace

3. Watch again at normal speed. Watch the words appear.
   - Ask: do captions appear too early, too late, or too fast? Do you finish reading before they disappear?

4. Turn on "Pause at each caption". Try a few captions.
   - Ask: does pausing help you keep up? Does it annoy you?

## Missed something

5. Skip past a caption on purpose, then press "Replay caption".
   - Ask: was that the caption you wanted back? Was it easy to find the button?

## Speed

6. Slow the video down. Watch one segment.
   - Ask: does slower help you read, or just make it boring? What speed feels right?

## Language

7. If they understand Kiswahili: switch captions to Kiswahili.
   - Ask: which language is easier to follow? Do the Kiswahili words match the speech?

## Reading on screen (contrast)

8. Try the different color modes / contrast.
   - Ask: which is easiest on your eyes? Any that are worse?

---

## Close

9. If you could change ONE thing so this felt natural, what would it be?

---

## Notes to capture (as they happen)

- What they had to ask twice for
- Where they leaned in / frowned / skipped ahead
- Any moment they said "wait" or "I missed that"
- Their own fix idea, word for word if possible

Do not fill this in from memory at the end. Write each note as it happens.
